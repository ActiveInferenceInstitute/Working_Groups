# Contributing

Thank you for helping shape the Active Inference Institute Working Groups.
Everything here is public, and so is every comment.

The draft [Charter](docs/working_group_charter.md) and
[Operating Procedures](docs/operating_procedures.md) are open to community
review, comment, and suggested changes, and to amendment before or at the
Charter Development Session of the 6th Applied Active Inference Symposium
(online, 12–14 November 2026).

## Comment on the founding documents

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/Working_Groups/issues/new)
that states:

1. **The provision**: the document and section, for example `Charter §3.4` or
   `Operating Procedures §3.3`. Starting the issue title with it helps others
   find related comments.
2. **The concern**: what is wrong, missing, or unclear, and why.
3. **The proposed change**: replacement text if you can, or the change that
   would resolve the concern.

This mirrors the structure of a formal objection under Charter §6.6, so a
comment can be carried into the Charter Development Session as written.

## Propose a text change

Pull requests against [`docs/`](docs/) are welcome for concrete wording
proposals. They are treated as comments: opening or merging a pull request does
not amend the Charter or Operating Procedures. The documents change only through
the adoption process they describe (provisional adoption at the Symposium and
ratification within 90 days) and, once ratified, through Charter Article X.
Maintainers may merge editorial fixes (typos, broken links, formatting) that do
not change meaning.

The position paper in [`paper/`](paper/) is a fixed copy of the published
Zenodo record ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.23066904](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23066904))
and is not edited here.

## Express interest in a Working Group

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/Working_Groups/issues/new)
naming the Field Working Group or a domain, and the role(s) you could fill:
active-inference expert, domain practitioner, or implementation maintainer or
user. This helps organizers plan; it is not by itself the written commitment
described in Charter §3.4.
