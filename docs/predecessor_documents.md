# Predecessor Governance Documents and Resources

*Version 1.0.0 · September 2026 · Active Inference Institute*

> **Source.** Appendix C of *Coordinating the Field: The Case for Ecosystem-Level Technology Roadmapping and Community Working Groups in Active Inference*, Active Inference Institute Working Paper Series, Version 1.0.0, September 2026, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23066904](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23066904). Referenced from the
> [Working Group Charter](working_group_charter.md) and the [Operating Procedures](operating_procedures.md).

The draft Charter and Operating Procedures in Appendices A and B draw on the governance frameworks of established technical coordination bodies. The following primary documents are publicly available and are commended to participants in the Charter Development Session for review prior to the November 2026 Symposium.

## IEEE Technical Activities Board Operations Manual (incorporating changes approved through the February 2023 TAB meeting)

IEEE Technical Activities Board Operations Manual (incorporating changes approved through the February 2023 TAB meeting). TAB structures for membership, officers, meeting notice, minutes, quorum, committee-member removal, Society publication authorization, and monitoring inform Articles III–V, VII, and X. Society Technical Committees are authorized in §4.2 but are not specified in detail. Pinpoints appear in the footnotes to Appendix A. The ieee.org URL below returned a non-text stub at retrieval; a June 2026 print-to-PDF lacked an extractable text layer. A readable February 2023 copy is at [https://ieee-sensors.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/tab-operations-manual.pdf](https://ieee-sensors.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/tab-operations-manual.pdf).

Available: [https://www.ieee.org/content/dam/ieee-org/ieee/web/org/tab/tab-operations-manual.pdf](https://www.ieee.org/content/dam/ieee-org/ieee/web/org/tab/tab-operations-manual.pdf)

## IETF RFC 2418

IETF RFC 2418: IETF Working Group Guidelines and Procedures. RFC 2418 establishes the governance model for IETF Working Groups, including the "rough consensus" decision standard and milestone-based accountability. The AII Charter's consensus-seeking model in Article VI and meeting procedures in Article V draw on RFC 2418.

Available: [https://rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2418.html](https://rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2418.html)

## W3C Process Document (2023)

W3C Process Document (2023). The W3C Process Document specifies the process by which W3C Working Groups develop Technical Reports. The AII Operating Procedures' deliverable development process (Section 3) and amendment cadence (Section 8) draw on W3C process structures.

Available: [https://www.w3.org/2023/Process-20231103/](https://www.w3.org/2023/Process-20231103/)

## IRS Form 1023 Sample Conflict of Interest Policy (Appendix A to Form 1023; text as reproduced by FREDLA)

IRS Form 1023 Sample Conflict of Interest Policy (Appendix A to Form 1023; text as reproduced by FREDLA). Article IX disclosure, recusal, minute-recording, and undisclosed-conflict review follow this sample. The COBIT landing page contains no operative disclosure or recusal text and is not a source for Article IX. The official IRS form mentions Appendix A but does not reprint the policy.

Available: [https://www.fredla.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/sample-conflict_of_interest-policy.pdf](https://www.fredla.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/sample-conflict_of_interest-policy.pdf)  and [https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-purpose-of-conflict-of-interest-policy](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-purpose-of-conflict-of-interest-policy)

## ANSI Essential Requirements (2010 edition via NIST; a 2025 ANSI edition exists but was not retrievable at citation)

ANSI Essential Requirements (2010 edition via NIST; a 2025 ANSI edition exists but was not retrievable at citation). Section 1.1 (openness; no undue financial barriers; voting membership not conditional on organization membership) informs Charter §§3.2 and 9.4. Section 2.7’s example consensus test—a majority of the body casting a vote and two-thirds of those voting, not counting abstentions—informs the two-thirds thresholds in Articles VI and X. ANSI ER does not contain a personal conflict-of-interest or recusal rule.

Available: [https://www.nist.gov/document/2010ansiessentialrequirementspdf](https://www.nist.gov/document/2010ansiessentialrequirementspdf)

## IETF RFC 5378

IETF RFC 5378: Rights Contributors Provide to the IETF Trust. Article VIII tracks RFC 5378 on employer or sponsor permission (§5.1), contributor retention of underlying rights (§5.10), and exclusive official publication of the adopted text (§§3.6, 5.9). CC BY 4.0 after publication, the exclusive AII imprint, and AII-POL-IP-001 (still in development) are proposal-specific. RFC 8179 (patent disclosure) and the W3C Patent Policy are not sources for Article VIII; that article has no patent clause.

Available: [https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5378](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5378)

## IEEE-SA Standards Board Operations Manual, clause 5

IEEE-SA Standards Board Operations Manual, clause 5. The IEEE-SA ballot rule is 75 percent Approve among Approve plus Do Not Approve with comment (§5.4.3.5), not two-thirds. Articles VI and X therefore do not take their supermajority from IEEE-SA ballot math. IEEE-SA remains a source for the idea of a recorded supermajority before an official output advances, and for sponsor review of work products. Available: [https://standards.ieee.org/about/policies/opman/sect5/](https://standards.ieee.org/about/policies/opman/sect5/)

Available: [https://standards.ieee.org/about/policies/](https://standards.ieee.org/about/policies/)
